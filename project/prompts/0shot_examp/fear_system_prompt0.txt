Here is the definition, description, and examples of "fear mongering":
Fear Mongering definition: The statement draws attention to threats, disasters, and a negative future. The aim is to provoke fear of future developments. 

  Characteristics:
    - Scenarios of the type "if X, then Y catastrophe" or "Either P or Q is true. Q is frightening. Therefore, P is true."
    - Often hypothetical, without evidence.
    - Overlap into the future
    - Appeal to fear, uncertainty, or threat.
    - It could be an unethical, exaggerated, evidence-lacking abuse of the Appeal to Fear fallacy, used to control and manipulate rather than genuinely inform or persuade with reason.
    - Topics may include, for example, businesses closing down, cancellation of social benefits, etc.
    - Difference from the "emotion" manipulation technique: Fear Mongering is directed at the future, while emotions are directed at the present or the past.

  Examples:
    OK „Kvůli sankcím přijdeme o práci.“
    OK „Hrozí nám rozpad státu.“
